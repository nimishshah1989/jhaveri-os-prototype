Building the rows above meant computing things that had previously been written by hand. Seven figures moved. Each was wrong before, not now.

One conflict I could not resolve, and did not paper over. Our own brokerage table books ₹15,895 of trail against her two Direct folios. A Direct plan pays a distributor nothing — that is the definition of Direct. The fee page prints ₹0 for both and says, in the client's own words, that our ledger is at fault and is flagged for correction. Either the seed is wrong or our brokerage engine is, and that is worth an hour of somebody's time before it reaches a real statement.

XII · What is honestly missing

A one-day change. Every price we hold is monthly — 44 dates from Jan-2023. So the per-fund month column is real and the one-day column is a dash with its reason printed. The market wire measures the same window and says which two days it used. A daily NAV feed turns all three on and changes no other line.

AUM, fund managers, minimum investment, instrument moves. Dashed, with the feed that owns each one named on the row.

Live rails. Every act prints its dated receipt today; the placed → debited → allotted lifecycle is shaped and labelled simulated. The language is finished. The plumbing is the real build.

Your COVID analysis. The Mirror's generator now writes six entries for any client from their own transaction history — patience, instalment discipline, every exit, the misses, a step-up counterfactual, the largest lumpsum. Your file would add its rule to that engine rather than replace it. It is still the one input I have asked for three times and do not have.
