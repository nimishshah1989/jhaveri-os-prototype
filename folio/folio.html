About this paper

The Daily Folio is written by your own records. Every figure is computed from your folios and the day’s official prices — nothing estimated, nothing invented. Where a number cannot be computed honestly, the Folio prints a dash and says why.

Your manager reads the same pages you do. The Folio indicates; it never advises — decisions are conversations, and conversations are Ravi’s craft.
